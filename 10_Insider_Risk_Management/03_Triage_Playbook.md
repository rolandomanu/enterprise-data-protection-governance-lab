Insider Risk Incident Triage Playbook

Purpose

Provides security analysts with a standardized workflow to investigate alerts generated by Microsoft Purview Insider Risk Management while maintaining legal and privacy compliance standards.

Investigation Steps

Step 1: Initial Alert Review

Access the Microsoft Purview compliance portal -> Insider risk management -> Alerts.

Review the aggregate risk score and identify the primary triggers (e.g., HR termination status + mass download).

Inspect the user's activity timeline to visualize the sequence of events leading to the alert.

Step 2: Contextual Analysis & False Positive Filtering

Check Role Relevance: Does the user's job description justify downloading these specific files? (e.g., Is a project manager handing off assets?).

Verify Timeline: Did the activity occur during standard working hours or late at night?

Review Content: Check if the affected files are genuinely sensitive (Confidential/Restricted) or public documentation.

Step 3: Escalation & Remediation Workflow

Level 1 (False Positive): Close the alert with appropriate documentation if the activity is verified as legitimate business operations.

Level 2 (Confirmed Policy Breach / Moderate Risk): Coordinate with the direct manager or HR business partner to review policy awareness.

Level 3 (Critical Exfiltration Risk):

Immediately notify Legal and Human Resources leadership.

Temporarily restrict user access permissions via Conditional Access / Entra ID if active data theft is suspected.
